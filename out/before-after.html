#3

cascade

tier 2 coachmark harness red

Why this rung. Stops at question 3 (is it a rule that changes what they should do, needed once, at a specific moment). Tier 0 cannot carry it: the payoff is read out of a settle event (kit.ts:cascadeRam banking RAM, surgeTierOf shattering locks or destroying a trap at higher counts) and there is nothing permanent on the grid to hang a label on between settles. Tier 1 is the wrong shape too: this is not a number the player consults on demand, it is a strategic instruction, hold the chain behind one unturned junction, then flip it together, that only matters the first time a bank actually lands. The known-drift table already flags that the shipped game's version of this exact coachmark states the wrong threshold (four nodes, cascadeRam pays from three), so whatever ships here has to be checked against the symbol, not copied from memory.

  • harness-red teach-sim fails on cascade teach-sim.before.txt lists cascade as uncovered with no waiver.
  • first-contact-day duel, day 1 the first legitimate three-node settle can happen as early as the first regular dive after the tutorial.
  • surface-budget duel has 2 of 4 moments and 0 of 2 firstSight used code_scan's budget.surfaces.duel shows room for a non-firstSight cascadeBanked moment without straining either cap.
  • resource-cost cascade banks RAM directly cascadeRam pays RAM into the next turn only on a big settle, so missing this is a foregone resource advantage every dive, not flavor.
