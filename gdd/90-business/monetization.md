# Monetization

> [!question] UNWRITTEN
> No monetization model has been decided. This note exists so the structure is complete and linkable, not because the design exists.

## What is already true

- **The entire full game is free and public** at kernel-panic.higgsfield.app, right now, with no gate of any kind.
- [[marketing-plan]] names that as **the single biggest commercial problem** in the project, and says it must be fixed before any marketing spend.
- The plan's goal is **8,000 Steam wishlists**, which presumes a paid Steam release.
- Those two facts are in tension and nothing currently reconciles them.
- The in-game [[credits|credit economy]] is entirely fictional. Nothing in the game touches real money.

## What this note must decide

- [ ] Free browser game forever, or a commercial release?
- [ ] If commercial: premium, free-to-play, or demo-to-paid?
- [ ] **What happens to the currently-public full build?** Cut it back to a demo, leave it and sell a different edition, or take it down?
- [ ] If a demo: which slice? The natural cut is the tutorial plus days 1 to 3, but that ends before [[day-8|horizon 3]], which is where the duel gets interesting.
- [ ] Price point, if any.
- [ ] Does the in-game credit economy ever touch real money? *(Strong default: no. The economy is a debt story and monetizing it would poison it. See [[credits]].)*
- [ ] Relationship to the ELVTR capstone framing. Does a course deliverable constrain commercial use?

## What argues for deciding soon

The Next Fest timing analysis in [[marketing-plan]] points at February to March 2027 and needs a store page live 5 to 6 months earlier, so roughly September 2026. A store page cannot exist without a price and a build boundary.
